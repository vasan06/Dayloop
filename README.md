# Dayloop
Dayloop helps you run your day as a loop: plan it, do it, review it. It combines tasks, notes, projects, habits, goals and a calendar in one offline-first app for Android and Windows. Your data stays encrypted on your device and syncs safely across devices, and when you miss a task, Dayloop recommends a new time and tells you why.
